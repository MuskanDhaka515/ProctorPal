# Campus Testing Center — Policy FAQ (sample data)

## Hours of operation
The testing center is open Monday through Friday from 8:30 AM to 5:00 PM, and Saturday from 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM. The center is closed on Sundays and on university holidays. The last exam of the day must start by 3:00 PM on weekdays and by 10:00 AM on Saturdays.

## Location and parking
The testing center is on the second floor of the Student Services building, room 210. Visitor parking is available in Lot C. Students should allow ten extra minutes to find parking during the first two weeks of the semester.

## What ID to bring
Students must show a valid photo ID before every exam. Accepted IDs are a current university student ID card, a driver's license, a state ID, or a passport. Expired IDs and photos of IDs on a phone are not accepted.

## Booking and rescheduling exams
Exams must be booked at least 24 hours in advance and no more than 30 days ahead. Each exam slot is two hours. Students can reschedule or cancel up to 12 hours before their slot at no penalty. Students who miss a booking without cancelling must contact their instructor before booking again.

## Late arrival
Students who arrive more than 15 minutes after their scheduled start time may lose their slot and will need to reschedule. Time lost from a late arrival is not added to the exam.

## Make-up exams
Make-up exams require instructor approval. The instructor must submit the exam and approval to the testing center before the student books. Once the exam is on file, the student can book a make-up slot like any other exam.

## Testing accommodations
Students with approved accommodations, such as extended time or a reduced-distraction room, should book through the testing center as usual and mention the accommodation when booking. Accommodations must be approved by the Office of Disability Resources first; the testing center cannot approve accommodations.

## Items allowed in the testing room
Phones, smartwatches, bags, notes, and food are not allowed in the testing room. Free lockers are available outside the room. Calculators and scratch paper are allowed only if the instructor permits them. Water in a clear bottle is allowed.

## Academic integrity
All exams are proctored in person and recorded on camera. Any suspected academic integrity violation is reported to the instructor.

## Contact a staff member
Students can reach testing center staff by email at testing@example.edu or by phone at (555) 010-2400 during open hours.
